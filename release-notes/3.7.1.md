A big one for a patch: every universe's characters and places brought up to the same level of detail, the app's universes now each in a folder of their own, and a way to choose which universes you use.

### Added
- **Choose which universes you use** (Settings → Universes): a checkbox per universe. Uncheck one and it leaves the Universe menu, Random, Clawd Royale and the screen saver, and new characters stop being cast from it; check it again and it's back as it was. At least one stays in.
- **Stormtroupers never hit anything.** Their bolts sail wide, as tradition demands.
- **Star Clawds changes places with more of the films' wipes**: seven kinds now, each with a soft edge.
- **Hover a card to find its character** in the window, and an option to show agents' tasks over the agents.
- A Ko-fi link for anyone who'd like to say thanks.

### Changed
- **Every character in every universe, redrawn with finer detail**: folds, collars, cuffs, belts and boots, seams and rivets, fur and hair in strokes instead of specks. Silhouettes, colours, weapons and every saved character stay the same. Star Clawds got the fullest refresh (Laya's and Mon Mothball's gowns stay floor-length).
- **Places filled in and fixed**: bare backdrops got their furniture (the Tentative IV, Larz Homestead, the Doom Moon and both throne rooms among them), and props that stretched on big screens (doors, towers, arches, hangars) now keep their size and stand on the floor.
- **Settings → Window** says what every option does, in one short line after each checkbox.
- **Each universe is one folder now**, with a template, `Tools/new-universe.sh` and `Tools/CheckUniverse/check.sh` for adding new ones, and the clawdify guide rewritten around them, including when a universe counts as complete. Nothing you see changes.

### Fixed
- A session waiting on its agents keeps moving and shows a bubble with how many are still out, instead of standing frozen.
- The session list stays sharp while the window changes universe (it used to break up into blocks during the animation).
- Clawd Slayer's Blondie Bear line no longer lands on a neighbour, and the Bronze no longer gets its name printed twice.

The full list of changes is in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#371-2026-10-19).

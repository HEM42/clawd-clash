Every character must read as standing on Clawd's four legs, whatever its costume or redesign. Fix other things the animation does, but never at the cost of the legs.

**Why:** in The Clawd Files' polish pass, shading the gaps between dark trouser legs (to hide the white outline there) merged each pair into one, and the user found Knowle Rohrer and Billy Miles looked two-legged: "make sure that the legs read as 4". A redesign that gave the Flukeman two stubby legs had to be undone too.

**How to apply:** when briefing helpers or redrawing a character, state that the four legs stay. Show before/after renders with BEFORE and AFTER written on them; unlabelled pairs confused the user. Related: [[clawd-clash-plan]].

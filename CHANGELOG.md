# Changelog

Clawd Clash's own history, newest first.

Its first three universes started as separate apps. Their changelogs from before they joined Clawd Clash are kept in `history/`:
- Star Clawds was **Clawd Wars**: [history/star-clawds.md](history/star-clawds.md) (up to 2.28.3)
- Marvelous Clawds was **Avengers: Clawd Wars**: [history/marvelous-clawds.md](history/marvelous-clawds.md) (0.1.0)
- Detective Clawds was **Justice League: Clawd Wars**: [history/detective-clawds.md](history/detective-clawds.md) (0.1.0)

Doctor Clawd, the fourth, was made for Clawd Clash ([doctor-clawd](https://github.com/rodclemen/doctor-clawd)), so its whole history is below, from 3.1.0.

- **Powers that aren't lightning no longer look like it.** Magic used to be drawn as forked lightning or a steady ray, so a telepath, a sorcerer and a sonic scream all looked like electricity. Now mind powers ripple out in rings (Jeanne Grae, Cassandra Supernova, Gorilla Grudd, Starrfish, Maxwell Laird, MOODOK, Sootekh, the Pusher, Gibson Prays and more), spells wind out as a twinkling ribbon of sparkles (Doctor Stranger, Scarlet Glitch, Lokee, Zatanya, Constantin, Circe, Enchantrix, Morgana, Baron Bordeaux, Wonk and more), and sound and force pulse out in arcs (Bansheen, Black Finch, Silver Crane, the Maestro, Gravitron). - **The Mad Milliner hypnotises instead of beaming.** Detective Clawds' Mad Hatter fought with a steady warm ray that read like lightning. His mind control is now a faint violet thread with rings rippling out along it, growing as they reach whoever he's working on. Any magic can use the new rings (`Magic.rings`).
- **The menu bar icon stays put on Random.** With Settings → Universe → Random, the icon used to change every time Random moved to another universe. It now keeps the icon of the universe you last picked yourself (remembered across launches), and changes again when you pick a single universe.
- **The blooper reel has its own section in the README**, under the universe trailers: the takes where the universes get mixed up, with a line about what it is and a link to the full 1080p version (`promo/clawd-clash-bloopers.mp4`, now committed like the main trailer). It used to be reachable only inside the full promo-plus-bloopers cut.
- **No two magic users' powers look exactly alike.** Every magic stream in every universe used to be drawn the same way within its kind, only the colour changing. Each character now gets a small variation of its own, numbered through all the universes within each kind: lightning differs in how many bolts fork off, how many kinks they have, how far they swing, side sparks and how fast it re-strikes; steady rays in thickness, wobble and bright beads running along them; rings, sound waves and spell sparkles in how many there are, how fast they go and their shape. `Tools/Preview` gains `magic` (every magic user's stream side by side) and `fightclip` (an animated fight, as an mp4).
- **E.Clawd no longer fires a beam.** In the film he never shot anything: he fights with just his glowing fingertip, and whoever he points it at glows softly, motes of light drifting up round them.
- **Doc Brawn no longer throws lightning.** He puts on the brainwave analyser from the first film (a metal cap of blinking bulbs and wires) and works a remote control, sending a little DeLorean racing along the ground at the foe on two trails of fire.
- **The park gates tower over the wall** at both window and screen-saver size, as in the film, and **the Jolly Roger is a whole galleon** moored at the quay at sunset (bowsprit and jibs, three masts, gun ports, a stern castle with lit windows, the skull flag, a gangplank), seen from the dock instead of from its deck.

## 3.2.0 (2026-10-15)

### Added
- **pi.** Clawd Clash can show [pi](https://github.com/badlogic/pi-mono) sessions, whichever model pi runs (Mistral, GPT, Gemini, local models…), with a pink outline and the model's name on the character. pi has no hooks, so connecting it writes a small extension of Clawd Clash's own to `~/.pi/agent/extensions/clawd-clash.ts`, which sends pi's events (session start and end, the prompt, each tool, the reply, compaction) to the hook; sessions already open load it after `/reload`. In Doctor Clawd, a pi session's Doctor regenerates when pi compacts its context, as a Claude Code one does. Nobody is assumed to use pi: the app offers it once, like the other AIs, only when it finds `pi` on the Mac, and Settings connects or removes it (removing deletes the extension). There's no "Needs you" for pi, since it never asks permission.

### Fixed
- **No more Homebrew deprecation warning.** Installing or upgrading `star-clawds`, `marvelous-clawds` or `detective-clawds` warned that `preflight` is deprecated; those casks now set their starting universe with `preflight_steps`, as Homebrew asks. The published 3.1.0 casks were fixed in the tap too, so the warning is gone already.

### Fixed
- **Qwen Code and Gemini CLI hook hints name the right hook again**: they said to enable `clawd-wars`, but the hook is called `clawd-clash`.
- **The screen saver's caption when no AI is running.** With no AI sessions running and Clawd Clash closed, the screen saver shows a made-up crew of locals, captioned CLAWD WARS ISN'T RUNNING · OPEN IT TO BRING YOUR CREW. That named the old app, and it was misleading: the screen saver works without the app (it reads the hooks), and opening the app brings no crew while no AI runs. It now reads NO AI SESSIONS RUNNING · THE LOCALS ARE HOLDING THE FORT.
- **A build run from source replacing your installed screen saver.** A copy of Clawd Clash run from `build/` (`./build.sh --no-install`) swapped the screen saver in `~/Library/Screen Savers` for its own build, as an app update would. It now leaves it alone, as it already left the AIs' hooks.
- **The screen saver's Options only ever keeping the first pick.** After installing, the universe chosen in Options stuck, and later picks were ignored until the screen saver was removed and installed again. macOS keeps the screen-saver host running between uses, and on recent versions a setting saved through ScreenSaverDefaults by one of its processes isn't seen by another that's already running. Options are now saved in a small file of the screen saver's own (`Clawd Clash Saver/options.json` in the host's Application Support), read afresh each time, so a new pick shows within a second.
- **Two Star Wars blasters firing the wrong shot.** Every Star Wars gun fired the same red bolt. Sebulba now fires the flame jet he used on Anakin from his pod racer, and the Jawa's ion blaster fires a blue ion bolt, like the one that drops R2 on Tatooine.
- **Marvel powers that looked wrong.** A check of all 193 Marvel characters' weapons: only Storm, Electro and Wiccan, who really throw lightning, still crackle with it. Thanos (the Power Stone), Doctor Doom, Dormammu, Mephisto, the Mandarin, Baron Mordo, Annihilus, Doctor Voodoo, Malekith and Mister Sinister now fire a steady stream of their colour instead of forked lightning. Emma Frost, a telepath with no blasts to throw, no longer fires a beam: she turns to diamond and fights hand to hand. The Leader was listed as "The MarvelVillain" (in the app and the README's roster image), left over from a rename.
- **A clean build from source**: `./build.sh` no longer warns about an unused value in the Deadpool fourth-wall transition (`ComicWipes.swift`).
- **The screen saver showing the wrong universe, or flicking between both.** The universe is now picked in one place, the screen saver's own Options: the app's Settings menu had its own pick, which always won, so choosing Marvel in Options still showed Star Wars. And with Random, the copies of the screen saver macOS runs side by side (one per display, one for System Settings' preview) each rolled their own universe and kept overwriting each other's, frame by frame; each now keeps its own.
- **The screen saver vanishing from System Settings' list.** On macOS 14 and later, the list of third-party screen savers comes from a background process (WallpaperLegacyExtension) that remembers what it found. A saver removed and put back while that process ran (a reinstall, an update, a removal) stayed missing from the list, although it was installed in the right place (`~/Library/Screen Savers`). Installing, updating and removing the screen saver now restart that process along with the screen saver host, so the list is rebuilt each time.

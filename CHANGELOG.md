# Changelog

Clawd Clash brings Clawd Wars (Star Wars), Avengers: Clawd Wars (Marvel) and Justice League: Clawd Wars (DC) together in one app. Their history before that is in [CHANGELOG-clawd-wars.md](CHANGELOG-clawd-wars.md) (up to 2.28.3), [CHANGELOG-avengers-clawd-wars.md](CHANGELOG-avengers-clawd-wars.md) (0.1.0) and [CHANGELOG-justice-league-clawd-wars.md](CHANGELOG-justice-league-clawd-wars.md) (0.1.0).

## 3.0.0 (unreleased)

### Added
- **Random universe.** **Random** (Settings → Universe) changes universe on its own every 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, to one of the other two; picking a universe stops it. In the window, a change of universe (Random's, or one you pick) now plays over the whole window, header and cards too, with the new universe's transition: one of Marvel's or DC's comic-book transitions, or Star Wars' edge or clock wipe. The crew of the new universe is already walking in underneath.
- **A third universe: DC.** Justice League: Clawd Wars joins as **DC** (Settings → Universe): 110 heroes and 97 villains, from the Justice League, the Bat-family and the Lanterns to Batman's and Superman's rogues, Apokolips and the Legion of Doom, and 43 places from the Watchtower and the Batcave to Oa and the Hall of Doom, every one drawn exactly as in Justice League: Clawd Wars. Arch-enemies face off first (Batman and the Joker, Superman and Lex Luthor, the Flash and Reverse-Flash…), the Javelin and the Batwing pick people up, the Bat-family swings on grey grapnel lines, Claude Code plays the heroes when another AI runs, and Superman's S-shield is the menu bar icon and the Status Symbols character. Changing place in DC uses its own fifteen comic-book transitions (the Speed Force, the Bat-Signal, Superman bursting through the page, the Lasso of Truth, a Joker card, Darkseid's Omega Beams and the rest), in the window and the screen saver. The screen saver's Options gain **DC**, and Random picks from all three. Coming from Justice League: Clawd Wars, Clawd Clash takes over the AIs it had connected, quits it, and starts in DC; the new `justice-league-clawd-wars` cask installs Clawd Clash starting in DC. `Tools/Transitions` renders DC's transitions between two DC places (`build/transitions out watchtower gotham`).
- **Two universes, one app.** Pick **Star Wars** or **Marvel** from the new **Universe** menu. A scene only ever shows one universe: switching recasts everyone (each character becomes the one in the same place on the same side of the other cast), moves the scene to one of the new universe's places, changes the menu bar icon (Vader's helmet or Captain America's shield) and the Status Symbols character, and new sessions and agents are cast from it. Star Wars brings Clawd Wars' 81 characters and 22 places; Marvel brings 193 characters and 43 places.
- **The screen saver's universe**: the screen saver's **Options…** button in System Settings picks the same universe as the app, Star Wars, Marvel, or Random (a new pick on each trip somewhere new). With several displays, each picks its own on Random.
- **Each universe keeps its own rules.** Star Wars: Han and Greedo face off first and Han shoots first, the Falcon picks Han up, whoever came first keeps their side when two AIs run, and Vader and Kylo never lead back to back. Marvel: arch-enemies face off first, the Quinjet and the Milano pick people up, flyers take to the air, and Claude Code plays the heroes when another AI runs.
- **The window steps aside when no AI is running.** In window mode, a few seconds after the last session ends and its crew has left, the window fades away instead of showing an empty scene, and it fades back (without taking focus) when an AI starts. Clicking the Dock icon, showing Clawd Clash from the menu bar icon or choosing Window Mode brings it back, and it then stays until something has run and finished again. A warning to read (an AI not connected, the status file unreadable) keeps it up.
- **Keep Characters on Top** (Settings, desktop mode): on, the crew stands above every window as before; off, it lives on the desktop itself, just above the icons and behind your windows. In window mode the same item is still Keep Window on Top.
- **A new icon**: a comic cover split into three slanted panels, one per universe (Sith red with stars, Marvel red and blue, Gotham night under the Bat-Signal), with CLAWD CLASH in big block letters across all three. Each panel colours its part of the name and the gutters cut through the letters. Spider-Man hangs upside down on his web over the name, Darth Vader raises his red saber and Batman squares up, all drawn by `Tools/MakeIcon` with the app's own sprites.
- **A screen-saver thumbnail for both universes**: the Clawd Clash title over Luke and Vader, Captain America and Thanos, in System Settings' screen saver list (it was the Avengers one).
- **Comic-book transitions in Marvel.** Moving to a new place in the Marvel universe (in the window and the screen saver) now uses a comic-book transition, a different one each time, instead of Star Wars' edge and clock wipes: halftone dots printing in the new place, a sound effect, three comic panels slamming down, a speed-line zoom through a white flash, or an iris closing on a "MEANWHILE, IN WAKANDA…" caption box. The sound effect belongs to someone on screen, each in its own look: SNIKT! (Wolverine, X-23) rips the old place open with three claw slashes, BAMF! (Nightcrawler) is a puff of purple brimstone, KRAKATOOM! or THOOM! (Thor) a storm-blue flash forking with lightning, THWIP! (any Spider-Man) a red web spinning out, BAROOM! (Hulk, the Thing) a green and orange gamma blast throwing rubble, and with none of them about, the classic yellow and red KAPOW! starburst. With Deadpool on screen he often takes over with two of his own: he climbs into the corner, tells you he's changing the set, and peels the old place off the screen like a comic page; or he surfs across on a giant chimichanga yelling CHIMICHANGAAAAA!, the new place in his wake. His captions turn up too ("MEANWHILE, ON A SMALLER BUDGET…"). Star Wars keeps its wipes. `Tools/Transitions` renders every transition as a clip and a sheet of stills (`FRAMES="key:0.3,0.6"` for single frames).

### Fixed
- **A build run from source replacing your installed screen saver.** A copy of Clawd Clash run from `build/` (`./build.sh --no-install`) swapped the screen saver in `~/Library/Screen Savers` for its own build, as an app update would. It now leaves it alone, as it already left the AIs' hooks.
- **The screen saver's Options only ever keeping the first pick.** After installing, the universe chosen in Options stuck, and later picks were ignored until the screen saver was removed and installed again. macOS keeps the screen-saver host running between uses, and on recent versions a setting saved through ScreenSaverDefaults by one of its processes isn't seen by another that's already running. Options are now saved in a small file of the screen saver's own (`Clawd Clash Saver/options.json` in the host's Application Support), read afresh each time, so a new pick shows within a second.
- **Two Star Wars blasters firing the wrong shot.** Every Star Wars gun fired the same red bolt. Sebulba now fires the flame jet he used on Anakin from his pod racer, and the Jawa's ion blaster fires a blue ion bolt, like the one that drops R2 on Tatooine.
- **Marvel powers that looked wrong.** A check of all 193 Marvel characters' weapons: only Storm, Electro and Wiccan, who really throw lightning, still crackle with it. Thanos (the Power Stone), Doctor Doom, Dormammu, Mephisto, the Mandarin, Baron Mordo, Annihilus, Doctor Voodoo, Malekith and Mister Sinister now fire a steady stream of their colour instead of forked lightning. Emma Frost, a telepath with no blasts to throw, no longer fires a beam: she turns to diamond and fights hand to hand. The Leader was listed as "The MarvelVillain" (in the app and the README's roster image), left over from a rename.
- **A clean build from source**: `./build.sh` no longer warns about an unused value in the Deadpool fourth-wall transition (`ComicWipes.swift`).
- **The screen saver showing the wrong universe, or flicking between both.** The universe is now picked in one place, the screen saver's own Options: the app's Settings menu had its own pick, which always won, so choosing Marvel in Options still showed Star Wars. And with Random, the copies of the screen saver macOS runs side by side (one per display, one for System Settings' preview) each rolled their own universe and kept overwriting each other's, frame by frame; each now keeps its own.
- **The screen saver vanishing from System Settings' list.** On macOS 14 and later, the list of third-party screen savers comes from a background process (WallpaperLegacyExtension) that remembers what it found. A saver removed and put back while that process ran (a reinstall, an update, a removal) stayed missing from the list, although it was installed in the right place (`~/Library/Screen Savers`). Installing, updating and removing the screen saver now restart that process along with the screen saver host, so the list is rebuilt each time.

### Changed
- **The last bat, lantern and JL marks in Detective Claws.** Batclaw Woman wears a red claw mark instead of the big red bat, like the rest of the Batclaw family, and throws a three-pointed clawarang instead of a batarang. Sinistro's chest carries the yellow lamp emblem of the other Corps, the Lantern memorial in Shore City has a green lamp on its plinth instead of the lantern circle, the Night-Glider's wing shows three black claw slashes in its yellow oval, and the Lance's tail fin reads CL instead of JL. The bat on the Batcave's computer screen and the Batmobile's fins stay as they are.
- **A Settings window, and a shorter menu.** **Settings…** (also ⌘, in the app menu) opens a window with every option in one place, with checkboxes, pop-ups and buttons: General (universe and Random, Dock icon, monochrome menu bar icon, launch at login, **Forget Idle Sessions**), Window Mode, Desktop Mode (size, characters on top, **Reset Position**), the AIs (each with Connect or Remove) and the screen saver. Window and desktop settings show side by side, whichever mode you're in. The right-click and menu bar menu keeps only Show/Hide, the mode, Location, Status Symbols, Settings… and Quit; the universe is picked in Settings.
- **The Star Wars cast is Clawd Wars' own, ported frame by frame.** All 81 characters were rebuilt on the shared pipeline and checked pixel by pixel against Clawd Wars' drawing code in hundreds of poses each. Star Wars also keeps Clawd Wars' gun handling: no gun raised while waiting or cheering, heroes check their guns without raising them, the flash stays at the resting muzzle, bolts run as they did, and each hero keeps its own practice rhythm (`Persona.soloFire`). The Emperor's crackle is pale blue and white again.
- **One engine for both**: every character in both universes is a `Persona` drawn through the same pose pipeline (`Pose.swift`), and each universe is a `Theme` in its own folder (`Sources/App/Universes/StarWars`, `.../Marvel`).
- **Upgrading from Clawd Wars or Avengers: Clawd Wars takes care of itself.** On launch, Clawd Clash takes over every AI the old app had connected: hooks that still run the old app's hook, and the AIs a Homebrew upgrade of the old cask disconnected. The old app, if it's still running, is quit. Clawd Clash starts in the universe of the app it replaces, and Star Wars keeps Clawd Wars' cast ids. (A copy run from `build/` doesn't take over; its banner says the AI still reports to the old app and to connect it from the menu.)
- **Menu**: "Show on Desktop" and "Show in Window" are now **Desktop Mode** and **Window Mode**, and **Universe** sits under Status Symbols.
- **New identity**: `Clawd Clash.app`, `com.rodclemen.clawdclash`, the `clawd-clash-hook` binary and `~/.claude/clawd-clash`.
